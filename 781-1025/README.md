## What changed vs 614 (rejected)
- **The whole route, no jump:** 614 used the 30 s preview (7 s on the Ancien Pont, then 140 m before the end). Now: six Studio
  shots at the six places to get right (right turn onto Boulevard de la Marina, Ancien Pont, left turn after the bridge, Carrefour
  La Béninoise straight on, Carrefour Abattoir U-turn, the last 110 m) joined by our own map that moves the car along every metre in
  between, naming each turn and roundabout as it passes. A live map with « Encore x km » in every Studio shot.
- **No floating trees or buildings:** they came from 614's capture method (it stepped the game's clock past its own « wait for the
  city » timeout), not from the game; the Studio waits until every chunk is built before its first frame.
- **The place is marked:** straight above the parked car, a red pin on the exact listing point with « Lagos Buka & Grills · C'est à
  votre droite », the RNIE 1, « Carrefour Abattoir · 200 m », « Pharmacie de l'Abattoir »; then a close-up map; then the whole
  route with a km-by-km itinerary; then the end card.
- **Names:** every label is from our data (OSM, landmarks, Henry's local names); the full check table with sources is in city-boy
  `docs/cloud/781-partner-video-redo/README.md` (branch `cloud/781-partner-video-redo`).
