# Ceety Boy · Your business on the map — Founding offer (100 places)

> English version (for Henry and English-speaking businesses). French version: `OFFER.md`. Same words as the price card ceetyboy.com/en/business-offer.

**Your business may already be on Ceety Boy.** Claim your page in 2 minutes, standing at your door, and get your Ceety Link:
the link that guides your customers to your door.

## 1. Free, forever, for every business
- **Your page on ceetyboy.com**: your pin on the map, your name, your category, the route to your door.
- **"This is my business"**: on your page, confirm the name, put the pin at your door, take a photo of the entrance.
- **Your Ceety Link**: send it on WhatsApp; your customer sees the way to your door, with the landmarks to look for on the way.
- **To print**: the poster (A4, A5, card) and the QR sticker (round, square, A4 sheet), to put up near the till.
